    Install a pack

    1. 1

      Download the pack

      Press Download and keep the file as a .zip. Don't unzip it.

    2. 2

      Open the pack folder

      In Minecraft, go to Options, then Resource Packs, then Open Pack Folder. Drop the .zip in.

    3. 3

      Turn it on

      Move the pack to the selected side of the list and press Done.

    Packs are made for specific Minecraft versions. If the game says a pack is for another version, you can still switch it on, but some textures may not show.
